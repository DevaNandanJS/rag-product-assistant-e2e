# Operations Advisory POLAR-OPS-02

**Issuer:** PolarNest Commercial Cooling  
**Related Products:** REF-320, REF-700  
**Date:** 2026-02-15  
**Subject:** Temperature Guidelines and Operational Limitations

REF-320 is intended for frozen storage and is documented at -18°C to -24°C.

REF-700 is a chilled display unit documented at +2°C to +8°C.

Temperatures depend on ambient conditions and loading. Daily energy consumption is not provided, and REF-700 is not described as medical-grade equipment.
