# Technical Advisory LOOM-TECH-03

**Issuer:** LoomCraft Industrial Machinery  
**Related Products:** TEX-12  
**Date:** 2026-03-01  
**Subject:** Stitching Capability and Standard Accessories for StitchPro ST-12

TEX-12 is a straight lockstitch machine for medium-weight woven fabric. It is not an embroidery machine and is not specified for heavy leather work.

The table and stand are included; extra presser feet are not included.

Online operator orientation is included.
