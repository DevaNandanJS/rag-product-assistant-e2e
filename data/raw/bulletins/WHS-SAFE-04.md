# Safety Advisory WHS-SAFE-04

**Issuer:** Docklane Material Handling  
**Related Products:** WHS-1800  
**Date:** 2026-02-01  
**Subject:** Load Distribution and Anchor Bolt Requirements for StackStore 1800

The WHS-1800 rating is 1,800 kg UDL for the entire bay. It is not a per-shelf rating.

Anchor bolts are required and sold separately.

The installer must verify concrete-floor suitability before the rack is used.
